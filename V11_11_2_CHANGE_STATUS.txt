NUNES RECRUITMENT CONSOLE V11.11.2
===================================

ALWAYS-ON / 24-7
- Start / Stop buttons removed from frontend.
- Recruitment automation is forced ON whenever the local service starts.
- Legacy Stop API no longer disables the always-on service; use STOP.bat to stop the application.

WHATSAPP
- WhatsApp Web auto-opens in the same approved Chrome profile.
- One-time QR link is reused after login.
- Status is visible in the top bar; Open WhatsApp remains a manual fallback in Settings.

HR INTERVIEW SELECTION
- HR chooses Top 20 / Top 30 / Top 40 / custom count per role.
- AI ranking remains continuous and evidence-based.
- No interview message is released until HR explicitly approves the batch.
- Batch approval immediately queues interview email + WhatsApp.
- Mon-Wed approval -> next-day interview.
- Thu/Fri approval -> Monday interview.

MESSAGING
- Stage 1 acknowledgement email + WhatsApp templates are editable.
- Stage 2 interview email + WhatsApp templates are editable.

UI
- Fonts increased slightly across Candidates, Sent, Roles, Messaging, Settings and Activity.
- Top-bar navigation retained.
